What is Taskmaster?

Taskmaster is a game of gloriously pointless challenges. Five contestants attempt to complete strange, creative, and often ridiculous tasks — usually worse than you'd expect, and occasionally better than anyone hoped.

The Taskmaster reviews every attempt, hands out points entirely at his own discretion, and crowns a champion. The most points wins. Fairness is optional.
